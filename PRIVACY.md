# OGDonutSMP Bot - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information the OGDonutSMP Bot collects when you use it and how we handle that data. By using the bot, you agree to this policy.

## 1. What Data We Collect
To make the bot work properly, we only store basic info needed for its features:
- Your Discord User ID and Server ID.
- Your Minecraft username or UUID (if you link your account).
- Bot-specific data like economy balances, playtime, stats, or open support tickets.
- Basic command logs to help us catch bugs, spam, or rule breaks.

We do **not** collect your IP address, email, password, or private DMs.

## 2. How We Use Your Data
We only use this info to run the bot, keep track of your server stats/rewards, and prevent abuse or exploits. We never sell, rent, or share your data with anyone outside our staff team.

## 3. Data Storage & Security
Your data is stored on our private database/server. We take reasonable steps to keep it safe, though no system online is 100% secure.

## 4. Deleting Your Data
If you want your data removed from the bot, you can open a support ticket in our Discord server or DM the bot owner. Keep in mind that deleting your data will also wipe your bot stats, balances, and linked account info.

## 5. Updates
We may update this policy if we add new features to the bot. Continuing to use the bot means you accept any changes made here.
